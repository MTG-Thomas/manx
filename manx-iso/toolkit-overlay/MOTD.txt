 --- Incident-response truths (learned expensively) -------------------------
  1. Breathe. Snapshot. Then act. In that order.
  2. `manx status` BEFORE anything else: know the box.
  3. Collect evidence before repairing the patient.
  4. Write down what you did BEFORE you do it. Future-you is a witness.
  5. The backup you have not restore-tested is a rumor.
  6. Two changes at once = two suspects.
  7. Destructive verb? Read it twice, then --i-know.
  8. Correlated failure at a fixed cadence? Look for a ticker, not a ghost.
